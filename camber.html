Camber

A car parts shop landing page with animated sections

A one-page landing page for a shop that sells car and bike parts. Good for: an auto parts store, a tyre and wheel shop or any online shop with a big catalogue. How it works: sections slide in as you scroll, product rows show trending wheels and spares, and adding a part makes its picture fly into the bag. Includes: a search box, brand logos, a parts catalogue, blog cards, short looping films and a full footer. How to use: swap in your own photos, products and text, then connect the search and the bag to your shop. Uses GSAP and Lenis for animation and smooth scrolling.
