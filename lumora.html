Lumora

A teacher's dashboard for the whole school day

A dashboard for teachers that puts the school day, classes and student work in one place. Good for: schools, tutoring centres and education apps. How it works: a bar shows today's lessons with a line for the current time, you take the register in a tap and drag students around a seating plan. Includes: classes, students, a timetable you drag lessons across, lesson plans, marking, a quiz builder, a gradebook, messages, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data with no backend, so add your own data or server. Built with TypeScript and GSAP, no framework.

Version history

  • v1.0.1, 2026-10-21 — New layout: tabs, sub-tabs and a dock. The sidebar is replaced by page tabs across the top (Today, Timetable, Classes, Teaching, Results, Messages, Library) with a dark pill that slides to the page you are on. Classes, Teaching and Results open a row of their own pages with live counts, and reopen the one you last had open. Fees, Leave, Ask Wren and Settings sit in a dark dock on the left with tooltips; on tablets and phones the tabs scroll in one strip and the dock becomes a bar along the bottom. A calm, solid page colour with solid cards, each page's key figures as soft colour tiles (deep tones in dark mode), the violet class colour replaced by clay, and Urbanist at the shared size scale.
