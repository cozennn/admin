Meridian

A dark investment dashboard with animated charts

A one-screen dark dashboard for tracking investments. Good for: fintech, wealth and investing apps. How it works: pick a time range and the main chart changes shape, hover it to see any day's value, and press Refresh for new numbers. Includes: a performance chart, a watchlist, asset mix bars, a risk gauge and market notes, dark mode only, on desktop, tablet and phone. How to use: replace the sample data in app.js with your own API, since there is no backend and the menu and filters are for show. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and uses GSAP.
