Coffer

An eight-page invoicing and accounting dashboard

A front-end dashboard for invoicing and accounting, made for freelancers and small studios. Good for: starting an invoicing, billing or finance app without building the screens yourself. How it works: you can create an invoice in a live editor, see it marked paid with a stamp, and track cash flow, payments and expenses. Includes: overview, invoices, invoice editor, single invoice, expenses, clients, reports for profit and loss, tax and late payments, settings, and light and dark mode. How to use: it runs on sample data, so connect your own backend, it uses GSAP for animation.
