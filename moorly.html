Moorly

An insurance broker portal with a stage route you can watch move

A broker portal for a marine insurance company, shown with sample data from a broker who places cover for ferries, fishing fleets, marinas, boatyards, cargo and charter boats. Good for: insurance brokers, agencies and underwriting teams who track applications, renewals, policies and commission in one place. How it works: each application's stages are drawn as a sea route, and once a stage's checklist is ticked and the underwriter's questions are answered, the button moves it on and a small boat sails to the next stop; the quote builder re-prices as you slide the insured value or pick a deductible and sends the new terms as a new version. Includes: a pipeline board you drag applications across, an applications table, a queue of open questions you answer in place, version history with a compare view, document uploads with missing files flagged, renewals by month with a remarketing board, book rolls where you sort each account, policies with endorsements and certificates, monthly statements with a pay flow and a paid stamp, commissions by month and program, integrations with API keys, a rule-based appetite check, a document library, training, settings, help, search with ⌘K, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data saved in the browser with no backend, and uploads stay in the browser, so add your own data and server. Built with React, TypeScript and GSAP.
