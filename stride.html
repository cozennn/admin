Stride

A personal fitness tracker dashboard with eleven pages

A fitness dashboard that shows activity, sleep, heart rate, food and workouts. Good for: a fitness app, a smartwatch companion app or a health product. How it works: log a workout and the activity rings, goals and history all update together, with an Undo button. Includes: overview, activity, heart, sleep, workouts, nutrition, a library of 33 exercises, progress, awards, goals, settings and light and dark mode. How to use: it comes with sample data, so replace it with your own users' data. No libraries needed.
