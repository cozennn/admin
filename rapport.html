Rapport

A sales CRM with a deal board, schedule and invoices

An eight-page sales CRM app for a team that sells to customers and sends invoices. Good for: a sales team, an agency or a service business. How it works: drag a deal to the next stage and it takes that stage's colour, and open any customer to see their history, tasks and contact details. Includes: overview, pipeline, customers, schedule, finance with invoices, growth, projects, settings, a quick actions bar and a phone layout. How to use: it comes with sample data, so replace it with your own backend. Built with TypeScript, HTML and CSS plus GSAP.

Version history

  • v1.0.3, 2026-10-17 — Steady on phones, solid menus. On phones the page can no longer be dragged sideways: anything that pokes past the screen edge is cut off instead of widening the page, and long words such as email addresses wrap. Menus, the notification and account panels, chart tooltips and dock labels now have a solid background, so the page never shows through their text. Nothing else changes.
  • v1.0.2, 2026-10-16 — Made for phones. On phones every action is in reach: the dock gains a More menu with favourites, log a call, copy link and CSV export. Forms open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen with Cancel and Save always in view, and detail panels slide up with a handle and a close button that stays in sight while you scroll. The dock fits the smallest phones, small phones held sideways get a slim side dock, tapped buttons no longer keep a hover tint, and nothing sits under the notch or the home bar. Desktop is unchanged.
  • v1.0.1, 2026-10-16 — Fills tall screens, bigger tap areas. On tall screens (portrait tablets, tall browser windows) the Pipeline board now fills the screen down to the Won and Lost targets instead of stopping halfway. On touch screens, links inside text and table rows (customer names, invoice numbers) get a full-size tap area, and the Schedule checkboxes are larger. Desktop layouts are unchanged.
  • v1.0.0, 2026-10-16 — First release. Rapport shipped, with the reel recorded from the real page.
