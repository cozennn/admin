Posy

A birthday card where you hand over a flower

An interactive birthday card with a hand-drawn cat holding a flower. Good for: a birthday surprise you send as a link to a friend or loved one. How it works: drag the flower to the stick figure and he turns around to take it. Then Happy birthday appears letter by letter with a burst of confetti. If you miss, the flower goes back, and Enter or Space hands it over for you. How to use: change the message in the HTML and share the page. No libraries needed.
