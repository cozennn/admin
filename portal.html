Portal

A Sign in button with a tiny door you walk through

A sign-in form whose Sign in button has a small door in it. Good for: a fun login screen for an app, a game or a creative site. How it works: press Sign in and the door opens, a small figure walks through and the door shuts behind them. The form then changes to a signed-in card, and Log Out plays it backwards. A wrong form shakes, and each step has a small sound with a mute button. How to use: copy the files into your site and connect your own login system. No libraries needed.
