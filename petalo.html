Petalo

A point of sale and back office for a shop with a till

A point of sale and back office for a small shop with one or more locations, shown with sample data from a florist with two shops. Good for: florists, gift shops, cafes and other counter shops that sell, take orders for later and manage stock and staff. How it works: you tap products onto a sale and take payment at the register, and the sale shows up straight away in sales, on the home screen, in the till's cash and in stock. Includes: a working register with card or phone pay and a tip prompt, cash with change, gift cards and an on-screen receipt with a PAID stamp, a home screen with takings by hour against last week and the day's pick-ups, an order board you drag between stages, sales and refunds, customers, reports with a busy-hours chart, payments and payouts, stock with use-by days and deliveries you check in, a staff rota you drag shifts across, tills you count and close, day closes and exports, settings, security, help, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data saved in the browser with no backend, so connect your own data, payments and server. Built with React, TypeScript and GSAP.
