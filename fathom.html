A dark crypto trading dashboard with live prices

A dark trading dashboard for a crypto exchange with eight pages. Good for: crypto exchanges, trading apps and fintech products. How it works: prices, the chart and the order book update live, and you can place and cancel buy and sell orders and set price alerts. Includes: Markets, Portfolio, Orders, Wallets, Alerts, Activity, Settings and Help, three currencies and four accent colours, on desktop, tablet and phone. How to use: replace the sample data in js/data.js with a real price feed, since there is no backend. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and uses GSAP for animation.

Version history

  • v1.0.0, 2026-10-06 — First release. First release: eight pages — Markets terminal, Portfolio, Orders, Wallets, Alerts, Activity, Settings and Help.
  • v1.0.3, 2026-10-06 — Candles grow in place. Each candle scales about its own body centre, so nothing slides up or down while a chart appears or re-cuts.
  • v1.0.2, 2026-10-06 — Live ticks glide. The liquidity map slides its new column in, the last-price tag and line move to the new level, axis labels fade when they step aside for a tag, and a price that leaves the chart's range cross-fades onto the re-scaled chart instead of re-laying out in one frame.
  • v1.0.1, 2026-10-06 — Smooth switches. Switching a pair, a range or the chart type is smooth: the chart, the volume strip and the depth strip fold and regrow together instead of cutting, the chart type cross-fades, and the header price lands on the new token instead of counting through the old one.
