Vitrea

An App Store publishing dashboard with an iPhone-style glass look

A dashboard for a small studio that sells apps on the App Store, with frosted glass cards and controls in the style of the iPhone. Good for: indie app makers, mobile studios and anyone tracking apps, releases and reviews. How it works: drag a release from build to TestFlight, App Review and live, and answer reviews from an inbox with quick replies. Includes: an overview with proceeds and downloads, a release board, app pages, a review inbox, subscribers and retention, product page tests, payouts, a team calendar, settings with a reduce transparency switch, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data with no backend, so add your own data or server. Built with TypeScript and GSAP, no framework.
