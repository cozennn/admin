Covry

A money and people back office with a cash chart you can filter by site

A back office for a business with a few locations, shown with sample data from a group of veterinary clinics. Good for: owners, practice or office managers and bookkeepers who look after cash, bills, payroll, staff and contracts in one place. How it works: the overview shows money in above a line and spending below it, month by month or week by week, and the columns reshape when you filter to one location; card payments that no rule could place wait in a stack, and you file the top one with one tap or tick “always” so every payment from that merchant sorts itself. Includes: transactions with filters and CSV export, company cards you can freeze or give a monthly limit, bills you pay or schedule, invoices you send, remind and mark paid with a paid stamp, four bank accounts with transfers, budgets against spend, sorting rules, tax filings you file and pay, a pay run you review and approve, timesheets, time off, shift timelines per location, a staff directory, a hiring board you drag candidates across, contracts you sign on a drawn signature pad, renewals, templates, a calendar, a report builder that saves and exports, a new-location checklist, settings, help, search with ⌘K, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data kept in the browser with no backend, and the suggested categories come from that sample data, so add your own data and server. Built with React, TypeScript and GSAP.
