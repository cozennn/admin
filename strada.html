A car rental landing page where cars drive in and out

A one-page landing site for a luxury car rental business. Good for: car rental, car sales or any vehicle booking service. How it works: pick a car class and the hero car drives off while the next one rolls in. The booking bar is a real form that searches and takes you to a filtered list of cars. Includes: fleet list, deals slider, partner logos, how it works, app section, a dark theme, a phone layout and all 10 images. How to use: change the cars and text and connect your own booking system, it uses GSAP and Lenis for animation.
