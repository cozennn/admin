Kestrel

A marketing analytics dashboard with eleven pages

A marketing dashboard for agencies and growth teams that shows website, ads and social results in one place. Good for: an agency client portal, a marketing tool or an in-house reports page. How it works: charts show a tooltip on hover, change smoothly when you pick a new date range, and visitor numbers tick up live. Includes: overview, SEO, paid ads, social media, data source pages, reports, settings, a quick search with Cmd or Ctrl+K, light and dark mode and a phone layout. How to use: replace the sample data in data.js with your own API. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript plus GSAP.
