Bamboo

A sign-in page where a panda covers his eyes

A sign-in page that starts dark, with a sleeping panda and a hanging lamp. Good for: a fun login screen for an app, a game or a kids or animal brand. How it works: pull the lamp cord and the room lights up, the panda wakes and the sign-in form rises. When you type your password he covers his eyes, and he only peeks when you tap show password. How to use: copy the files into your site and connect the form to your own login system. No libraries needed.
