Moonart

A space-themed gallery with panels that open on hover

A two-screen gallery page with a space hero and a row of six image panels. Good for: an art or photo portfolio, a music page or a creative studio. How it works: a moon floats over starry layers that follow your mouse. Hover or click a panel and it opens wide while its title appears and the accent colour changes. On desktop the panels also move on their own. How to use: swap in your own images and titles. No libraries needed.
