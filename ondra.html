Ondra

A marketing and sales dashboard for brands that sell online and wholesale

A marketing and sales dashboard for a brand that sells online and to shops, shown with sample data from an outdoor gear company. Good for: marketing teams, founders and agencies who track campaigns, channels, budgets and wholesale leads. How it works: the home screen shows revenue, orders, conversion and return on ad spend, and picking one redraws the chart, with each campaign launch marked on it. Includes: a campaign list and a calendar you drag campaigns across, monthly budgets with sliders that move money between channels, a lead inbox and a board you drag leads to won, lead details with a call and email log, channel, funnel and attribution reports, audiences built from simple rules with a live size estimate, a report builder with CSV export, settings with team roles, help, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data with no backend, and the connected services in settings are switches, so add your own data or server. Built with TypeScript and GSAP.
