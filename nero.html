Nero

A product picker page where each chocolate bar flips in

A one-screen product page for a chocolate brand with four bars to choose from. Good for: a chocolate, coffee or snack shop, or any brand with a few product flavours. How it works: click a small picture or press the arrow keys and the bar turns away while the next one swings in. The background colour and text change to match, and cocoa beans float around the cursor. How to use: swap in your own products, photos and colours, it uses GSAP for animation.
