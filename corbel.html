Corbel

A sales dashboard for shops that sell online and in store

A thirteen-page dashboard for a shop that sells both online and in its own stores. Good for: a retail brand, a home goods shop or an online store builder. How it works: the dashboard builds itself on load, tabs switch every chart between all sales, online and in store, and a colour setting turns the whole app from black and white to Pastel, Ocean or Sage, in light or dark. Includes: dashboard, products, orders, customers, messages, email, automations, analytics, integrations, project boards, help, feedback, settings, CSV export and a phone and tablet layout. How to use: it comes with sample data, so replace it with your own backend. Built with TypeScript, HTML and CSS plus GSAP.
