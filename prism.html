Prism

A live sales dashboard for an online store

A one-screen dashboard that shows an online store's sales and traffic live. Good for: a shop owner's control room, a wall screen in the office or a store analytics product. How it works: about once a second a new sample order arrives and the charts update, and you can switch between Live, 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days. Includes: eleven panels like revenue, traffic, regions and signups, pause and refresh buttons, CSV export, full screen, dark and light mode and a phone layout. How to use: connect your real data feed in data.js. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript plus GSAP.
