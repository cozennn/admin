Norda

A one-page furniture store that animates as you scroll

A one-page store template for a furniture, interior or home goods brand. Good for: showing a product range on a single page with a strong first impression. How it works: the page opens with the main product growing into place, and pressing plus flies the product into the cart. Includes: hero, new arrivals, categories, best sellers, a room scene, reviews, a contact footer, 19 photos and a phone layout. Note: there is no backend, cart page or checkout. How to use: edit products and text in the HTML, it uses GSAP and Lenis for animation.
