Solvik

A sales CRM in frosted glass with a deal board and invoices

An eight-page sales CRM app in a frosted-glass look, for a team that sells to business customers and sends invoices, shown with sample data from a company that sells and installs solar panels. Good for: a sales team, an installer or a service business that tracks deals from first call to paid invoice. How it works: drag a deal to the next stage on the board and its card takes that stage's colour, open any customer to see their deals, tasks and contact details, and mark an invoice paid to see a PAID stamp land on it. Includes: an overview with revenue against a target and a quota ring, a deal board with a list view and a deal panel, a customer directory and profiles, a month and week schedule you drag tasks across, finance with cash flow and invoices you can download, growth reports with CSV export, installation projects with checklists, settings, search across everything, a quick actions bar, dark and light mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data saved in the browser with no backend, so replace it with your own. Built with TypeScript, HTML and CSS plus GSAP.
