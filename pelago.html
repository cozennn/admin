Pelago

A sales dashboard for a small online shop

A sales dashboard for a small brand that sells online, in its own shop and to other stores. Good for: small shops, makers and e-commerce businesses. How it works: a chart compares each month's sales with last year, and you drag orders across a board from packing to shipped. Includes: 10 pages with orders, refunds and returns, products and stock, customers, marketing, finance, reports, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data with no backend, so add your own data or server. Built with TypeScript and GSAP, no framework.
