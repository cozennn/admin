Vesper

A studio website where scrolling plays the sunset

A one page website for an architecture studio with a video that moves as you scroll. Good for: an architect, interior designer, real estate brand or any studio that sells a mood. How it works: Scroll down and the hero video plays forward, from sunset to night, while text sections appear one by one. Scroll up and it plays back. How to use: Replace the video, photos and text with your own. Uses GSAP and Lenis for smooth scrolling and animation.
