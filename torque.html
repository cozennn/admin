Torque

An admin panel for a car repair workshop

An eight-page admin app for a car repair garage to run its bays, jobs, parts and invoices. Good for: a car garage, a tyre shop or a vehicle service center. How it works: see every bay live with its car and timer, drag jobs from check-in to ready for pickup, and send estimates for the customer to approve. Includes: workshop floor, jobs, schedule, customers, parts, invoices, reports, settings and a phone layout. How to use: it comes with sample data, so replace it with your own backend. Built with TypeScript, HTML and CSS plus GSAP.
