Ledge

An inventory and sales dashboard with a shop register

A seven-page dashboard for a shop that sells in store and online. Good for: a small retail shop, a cafe or anyone who needs to track stock and sales. How it works: tap products in the register to build a sale, charge it, and the stock goes down on every page. Includes: overview, register, orders, stock, purchasing, reports, settings, CSV and JSON export, light and dark mode and a phone layout. How to use: it comes with a year of sample data saved in the browser, so connect your own API in src/model.ts. Built with TypeScript, HTML and CSS plus GSAP.
