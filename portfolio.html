Portfolio

An animated developer portfolio built with React and Tailwind

A dark one-page portfolio website for developers and designers. Good for: showing your work, skills and contact details to clients and employers. How it works: your name flies into place on load and your photo forms from small tiles that move with your mouse and scroll. Includes: an about section that lights up as you read, services, project case studies, a shelf of video clips, a playable mini game and contact buttons. How to use: put your name, links and projects in one settings file, it uses React, Tailwind and GSAP.

Version history

  • v1.0.1, 2026-10-15 — A particle portrait. After the intro, thousands of tiles cut from your project screenshots gather out of the air into your face (WebGL2, one draw call). The tiles part under the cursor and blow apart as you scroll, then come back. On desktop the word drops below the face so nothing crosses it. Reduced motion shows the finished face at once; without WebGL2 the plain photo is shown.
  • v1.0.0, 2026-10-09 — First release. One React app — the thrown-name intro, about, services, work, the reel shelf, a playable headline and contact — with the reel recorded from the real page.
