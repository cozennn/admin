Talvo

A finance app for a small business with a cash forecast

A money app for a small business that shows where its cash will be in the next 90 days. Good for: small companies, finance teams and fintech app makers. How it works: a chart shows your future balance, and you drag bills and invoices to other days to see the line change. Includes: 13 pages with bills and approvals, invoices, company cards you can freeze, payroll, budgets, savings, month-end tasks, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data with no backend, so add your own data or server. Built with TypeScript and GSAP, no framework.
