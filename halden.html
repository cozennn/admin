A six-page sales CRM with a drag-and-drop deal board

A front-end sales CRM app for teams that need a working pipeline to build on. Good for: starting a CRM, sales tracker or client management tool. How it works: drag a deal from one stage to the next and the totals and dashboard numbers update right away. Click a contact to open their profile. Includes: dashboard, pipeline board, contacts, activities, reports with charts, settings, light and dark mode, four accent colours and a phone layout. How to use: it runs on sample data with no backend, so connect your own, it uses GSAP for animation.
