Mavora

An online store dashboard with sales and order tracking

A dashboard for running an online store, from today's sales to packing orders. Good for: coffee roasters, food brands and other small online shops. How it works: a chart shows sales by channel against a daily goal, and you drag orders across a board from paid to shipped. Includes: 8 pages with orders, products and stock, customers, an inbox, reports, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data with no backend, so add your own data or server. Built with TypeScript and GSAP, no framework.
