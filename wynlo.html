Wynlo

A sales CRM with a drag-and-drop deal board and a country map

A sales CRM app for a brand that sells online, on marketplaces and to shops, shown with sample data from a skincare company. Good for: a sales team or a growing brand that tracks deals, orders and customers in one place. How it works: drag a deal to the next stage on the board and the totals update, switch the twelve-month chart between revenue, profit and orders and the bars change shape, and click a country on the map to see what it buys. Includes: an overview with monthly sales by channel against a target, four headline figures, a map with one square per country, a deal funnel, best sellers, today's tasks and the latest orders, a deal board with a list view, a deal panel and a forecast, about 2,000 sample orders with filters, bulk updates and a refunds queue, customer accounts with a health score, the people at each account, customer segments, products with stock levels and restocking, tasks, reports with CSV export, a team leaderboard, settings, help, search with ⌘K, forms to add deals, orders, accounts, people and tasks, a sidebar that folds into a slim icon bar, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data saved in the browser with no backend, so replace it with your own data and server. Built with React, TypeScript and GSAP.
