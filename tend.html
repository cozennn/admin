Tend

A clinic CRM with a WhatsApp inbox and patient board

A seven-page app for a clinic front desk to manage patients, visits and WhatsApp messages. Good for: a family clinic, a dentist, a physio or any practice that books appointments. How it works: move patients from expected to waiting to seen with one button, and send visit reminders by WhatsApp in one click. Includes: today board, WhatsApp inbox, patients, schedule, campaigns, reports, settings, light and dark mode and a phone layout. How to use: it comes with sample data and sending is only simulated, so point src/whatsapp.ts at your own server that uses the WhatsApp Cloud API. Built with TypeScript, HTML and CSS plus GSAP.
