Selvar

A sales dashboard and CRM for teams that sell to businesses

A sales dashboard and CRM for a team that sells to other businesses, shown with sample data from a solar company. Good for: sales teams, founders and agencies who track deals, leads and a quarterly target. How it works: the home screen shows how close the team is to its target and what to do next, and you drag deals between stages on a board. Includes: a quarter-to-target bar with a forecast, a Copilot card and chat that answer set questions from your own data (rule-based, ready to connect to your own AI service), a deal board and a deal list, a forecast you adjust deal by deal, deal details with notes, lead scoring with sliders, a week calendar you drag meetings in, contacts and companies, a report builder with CSV export, team targets, settings, help, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data with no backend, so add your own data or server. Built with React, TypeScript and GSAP.
