Sorrel

A one-page skincare store where bottles jump into the bag

A one-page store template for a skincare or beauty brand. Good for: a natural skincare, cosmetics or wellness shop. How it works: shoppers drag through the products and press Add to cart to send the bottle flying into a real slide-out bag with quantities and a total. Includes: product row, story section, reviews and a phone layout. Photos: the demo photos are not included, you get same-size placeholders and links to the originals. How to use: add your own photos and text, it uses GSAP and Lenis for animation.
