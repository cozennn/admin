Bundles

Playful scenes

Interactive scenes that make a page memorable: hatch a dragon, shake a snow globe, turn one tree through four seasons, tap a day to change the sky, wake a lamp, steer a reptile cursor, and read the time off a brass spider.

  • Ember — Hold the egg to hatch a tiny dragon
  • Snowglobe — A snow globe you can shake to make it snow
  • Solstice — Drag a slider to move one tree through the seasons
  • Weather — A glass weather card that changes with each day
  • Lamp — A cute desk lamp that wakes when you pull its cord
  • Reptile — A line-drawn lizard that crawls after your mouse
  • Arachne — A live clock whose hands are a spider's legs
