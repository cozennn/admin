Login & sign-up forms

Sign-in and sign-up forms people remember: a panda that covers its eyes for your password, a cat that peeks, liquid glass, a door that opens, a password that hatches a guardian. Each is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript you can drop into any project.

  • Bamboo — A sign-in page where a panda covers his eyes
  • Mochi — A sign-in page where a cat covers her eyes
  • Den — A sign-in page where a husky puppy covers his eyes
  • Pebble — A round glass sign-in form for login and sign up
  • Flow — A liquid sign-in page that ripples as you type
  • Lunara — A glass sign-in page over a desert at night
  • Nocturne — A glass sign-in page over a forest at night
  • Portal — A Sign in button with a tiny door you walk through
  • Vault — A sign up form where the lock grows with your password
  • Huddle — A sign-in form with shapes that watch you type
  • Verso — A sign-in card that sweeps over to sign-up
  • Tether — A login button that runs away until the form is filled
  • Warden — A password strength meter with a growing dragon
  • Lumen — A sign-in page with a pull-cord light bulb
