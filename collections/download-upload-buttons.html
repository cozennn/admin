Download & upload buttons

Buttons that turn waiting into something to watch: the outline becomes the progress bar, the arrow becomes the water, a glass fills, a parachute drops the file. Download, upload and submit — each one shows its progress in a different way.

  • Parachute — A download button with a parachuting arrow
  • Bead — A download button whose outline is the progress bar
  • Brim — A download button that fills up like a glass
  • Ripcord — A download button that turns into its own progress bar
  • Silo — A download button that opens a box and fills it
  • Thrum — A download button whose arrow becomes a moving wave
  • Tide — A download button whose arrow lies down and becomes the file
  • Keel — An upload button whose arrow becomes a water level
  • Plimsoll — An upload button that fills with liquid as it uploads
  • Quench — A submit button that fills up as it sends
