Navigation bars

Bottom navs, tab bars and docks where the indicator is the show: a drop of liquid, an electric arc, a filament of light, a word that becomes the dock.

  • Meniscus — A bottom tab bar that moves like liquid
  • Lucid — A glass tab bar with a sun and moon theme switch
  • Crest — A tab bar with a liquid drop marker
  • Surge — A navigation menu with an underline that jumps like electricity
  • Opal — A glass navigation bar with a soft stretchy highlight
  • Arc — A bottom tab bar with a ring of light that stretches
  • Relay — A contact menu where the word CONTACT opens your links
