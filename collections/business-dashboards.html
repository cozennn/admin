Bundles

Business dashboards

Forty-one dashboards for running a business: Halden, a six-page sales CRM with a drag-and-drop pipeline; Tandem, a sales workspace where you work a ranked lead queue and every lead score explains itself; Fernly for projects across seven pages; Coterie for people ops and hiring; Wharf for stock and orders; Ledge, a shop's point of sale, stock and purchase orders, where a sale flies into the ticket and prints its receipt; Parley, a two-way inbox for customer messages with campaigns, automations and reports; Rapport, an eight-page sales CRM where a deal takes its stage’s colour as you drag it and every customer has a profile of their deals, tasks and details; Sprig, a twelve-page studio CRM where a deal is dragged into Won, with companies, contacts, meetings, email and a report builder; Mender, a support-desk CRM where cases drag between lanes and you assign each case’s steps by dragging people onto them; Accord, an eight-page client-pipeline CRM whose deal cards lean as you drag them between stages and open dark to show the contact; Tend, a seven-page clinic CRM with a WhatsApp inbox, patient records, a clinician schedule and reminder broadcasts; Corbel, a thirteen-page sales dashboard for a shop that sells online and in store, with products, orders, customers, an inbox, email campaigns, automations and analytics; Lintel, a real-estate CRM where you set a listing's price on a gauge with two sliders, with homes for sale and to rent, a deal board that stamps SOLD, scored leads and a live chat; Hearth, a twelve-page mortgage CRM for loan officers, with leads, loan files, a document vault and a pipeline where a funded loan floats its commission up; Torque, an eight-page admin panel for a car repair workshop, with a live floor of bays and lifts, a job board, repair orders with a digital inspection and customer-approved estimates, a bay planner, parts and invoices; Pane, a seven-page team workspace in liquid glass, with home widgets and activity rings, a focus timer that runs in a capsule at the top, a drag-and-drop task board, a calendar, projects and insights; Ivora, a fifteen-page dental practice app with live chairs, an interactive tooth chart, a chair planner, treatment plans, prescriptions, lab cases and insurance claims; Cadra, an ERP for a bike maker with a drag-and-drop Gantt production schedule, work orders, a sales board, a showroom till, purchasing, three warehouses, shipments, people and payroll, a ledger and invoices; Tavola, a nine-page restaurant operations dashboard in glassmorphism, with a kitchen board you drag tickets across, a floor plan you seat guests on, bookings, menu engineering, stock, a staff rota and sales reports; Lumora, a seventeen-section teacher's dashboard for a school, with a day ribbon, registers, a drag-and-drop seating plan, a timetable, lesson plans, a marking queue, an AI quiz builder and a gradebook; Sunrow, a sixteen-section field ERP for a solar and battery-storage builder, with a drag-and-drop Gantt schedule, projects, contractors, materials and purchase orders, budgets, approvals, timesheets, safety and equipment; Vessa, a nine-section inventory app for a skincare lab, with stock across three locations, production batches with QA release and expiry, a pick-pack-ship order board, purchasing, bin maps and analytics; Noria, an eight-page workflow-automation app for a freight forwarder's ops desk, with a live run pulse, a node-canvas flow builder with test runs and failures grouped by cause; Mavora, an eight-page store dashboard for a coffee roaster, with takings by channel against a daily goal, a live order feed, a roast queue and a fulfilment board you drag orders across; Pelago, a ten-page sales console for a ceramics studio, with demand by category month by month against last year, a 24-hour order dial, a packing bench you drag orders across and kiln firings; Pembra, a nine-section people, rota and payroll app for a two-hotel group, with a live shift pulse, a drag-and-drop rota, timesheets, time off, hiring and a monthly pay run; Kelvo, a nine-page people, rota and payroll app for three climbing gyms, with an hourly coverage strip on a drag-and-drop rota, suggested cover for open shifts, timesheets, time off, hiring and a pay run that stamps PAID; Folka, a ten-section people, rota and payroll app for a coffee company, with a colour-block overview, a drag-and-drop rota with an hourly coverage row, staff photos, time off, hiring and a monthly pay run; Selvar, a ten-section sales dashboard and CRM for a solar company, with a quarter-to-quota runway, a drag-and-drop deal board, a forecast you adjust deal by deal, lead scoring and a rule-based Copilot; Petalo, a florist's point of sale and back office, with a working register, an order board you drag across, stock with vase life, a staff rota and tills you count and close; Olvara, a twelve-page back office for a skincare brand, with a product shelf by collection, a courier pickup countdown, a lab board you drag batches into stock, a cure schedule, orders and returns, stockists and a team rota; Ondra, an eight-section marketing and sales desk for an outdoor gear brand, with a performance chart you switch by its headline figures, a campaign calendar you drag to reschedule, budgets, a wholesale pipeline you drag to Won and channel, funnel and attribution reports; Ambrel, an eleven-section point of sale and back office for a homewares shop, with a register where a product flies into a till-roll ticket, members with tiers and points, a printed receipt, refunds by line, a cash-drawer count, deposits, stock with reorders and closed-day reports; Tessly, a thirteen-section sales CRM for a stone and tile studio, with client files and a touchpoint timeline, a pipeline ladder, a deal board you drag to Won, a quarter forecast, projects, invoices and commissions; Solvik, an eight-section sales CRM in frosted glass for a solar installer, with a drag-and-drop deal board tinted by stage, customer profiles, a schedule, invoices that stamp PAID and growth reports; Kerfa, a ten-section sales dashboard in frosted glass for a furniture workshop, with revenue against last year that morphs by range and channel, a deal board where Won books a real order, a quote builder, orders with deposits, accounts and quotas; Moorly, a ten-section broker portal for a marine insurer in frosted glass, with application stages drawn as a sea route a boat sails along, a quote builder, a pipeline board you drag, renewals, book rolls, policies with endorsements and certificates, and agency bills; Wynlo, a twenty-page sales CRM for a brand selling online, on marketplaces and to shops, with a deal board you drag, a forecast, a country map and about 2,000 orders with a refunds queue; Merrin, a twelve-page sales CRM for a home-goods studio, with a sales chart you switch by headline figure, a deal board you drag, orders packed in bulk and a photo catalogue with stock; and SOLUM, a store admin dashboard for orders, products and customers that ships with the storefront it runs. Each moves as one product and is ready to wire to your own data.
