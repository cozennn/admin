Shop templates

Complete storefronts you can rebrand and launch: SOLUM, a nine-page trail-apparel store with a scroll-played hero film, a full checkout and an admin panel, in TypeScript; KVIST, a bento-grid home-goods store with sign-in and a live admin dashboard, in React, Tailwind and Firebase; Kessler, a six-page leather-goods shop on a modernist grid; Norda, a one-page Nordic home-goods store that moves like a film; Sorrel, a botanical skincare shop where every bottle tosses itself into the bag; Sola, a footwear atelier whose cart is a real shopping bag; Oria, a furniture store with a scroll-film home page, a filterable shop, product pages, a bag and a working checkout, in TypeScript; and Camber, a performance-parts store whose hero swaps between five vehicles and whose build film plays as you scroll. Kessler, Norda, Sorrel, Sola and Camber are plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

  • Kvist — A home goods store with an admin dashboard
  • Kessler — A six-page online store with a working shopping bag
  • Norda — A one-page furniture store that animates as you scroll
  • Sorrel — A one-page skincare store where bottles jump into the bag
  • Sola — A shoe shop where the cart is a real shopping bag
  • Solum — A clothing store website with checkout and an admin dashboard
  • Camber — A car parts shop landing page with animated sections
  • Oria — A furniture store with shop, product pages, bag and checkout
