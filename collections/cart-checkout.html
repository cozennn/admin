Cart & checkout

The moments that close a sale: an item that hops into a cart that comes for it, a pay button that looks at you, a card that swipes through, a checkout where the card is the form — plus two shop pages to put them in.

  • Caddy — An Add to cart button where a cart drives off
  • Cart — A Buy Now button that turns into a delivery truck
  • Dash — A round Add to cart button where the cart drives away
  • Depot — An order button where a van picks up your parcel
  • Glance — A Pay button that checks your face before paying
  • Tender — A pay button that swipes a card through itself
  • Sable — A checkout form with a card that fills in as you type
  • Atelier — An add to cart button that packs the item into a bag
  • Sola — A shoe shop where the cart is a real shopping bag
  • Dispatch — A Buy Now button that packs and ships the order
