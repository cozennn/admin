Buttons & toggles

Everyday controls with a flourish: a toggle that fills instead of lighting up, a theme toggle the new light floods out of, a favourite button that mints its own star, buttons whose label is the moving part, a liquid hover, and soft-UI buttons that grow into their own controls.

  • Decant — Glass toggle buttons that fill with liquid when pressed
  • Lumen Toggle — A dark mode switch that spreads the new theme
  • Gild — A favourite button that makes a new shiny star
  • Glyph — Buttons whose letters move when you hover
  • Swell — A button that fills with colour like a liquid on hover
  • Relief — Soft buttons that grow into a player and share menu
