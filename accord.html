Accord

A client CRM with a drag-and-drop deal board

An eight-page CRM app for a sales team to follow customers from first lead to won deal. Good for: an agency, a service company or a B2B sales team. How it works: drag a customer card from one stage to the next, with undo, and open any customer to see their deal, notes and tasks. Includes: customers board, customer profiles, dashboard with charts, tasks, activity, team, projects, settings, a sign-in screen, CSV export and a phone layout. How to use: it comes with sample data, so replace it with your own backend. Built with TypeScript, HTML and CSS plus GSAP.
