Pembra

A staff, shifts and payroll app for hotels and shift teams

An app for managing staff shifts, attendance and pay for a business with several teams. Good for: hotels, restaurants, care homes and other shift-based businesses. How it works: a live chart shows who is working, late or off sick, and you tap a shift to clock someone in. Includes: a drag-and-drop shift plan, timesheets, time off, hiring, payroll with payslips, reports, an org chart, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data with no backend, so add your own data or server. Built with TypeScript and GSAP, no framework.
