Sleuth

A 404 page where your mouse is a magnifying glass

A page not found screen with a detective bird. Good for: the 404 error page of any website or app. How it works: your mouse becomes a magnifying glass and the bird walks to wherever you point. Hidden notes on the floor show up only under the glass. How to use: copy the files into your site, set it as your 404 page and change the text and links. No libraries needed.
