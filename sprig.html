Sprig

A twelve-page CRM for small agencies and studios

A CRM app for a small agency or studio to manage clients, deals, meetings and email. Good for: a design studio, an agency or a freelancer team. How it works: drag deals across stages on a board, drag meetings on a calendar, and build your own reports by picking what to show. Includes: dashboard, deals, notes, emails, reports, workflows, companies, contacts, meetings, projects, settings, a help center and a phone layout. How to use: it comes with sample data, so replace it with your own backend. Built with TypeScript, HTML and CSS plus GSAP.
