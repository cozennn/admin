Tavola

A restaurant dashboard with a kitchen board and floor plan

A nine-page back office app for a restaurant, with a frosted glass look. Good for: a restaurant, bistro or cafe, or a company that builds restaurant software. How it works: drag orders across the kitchen board, drag waiting guests onto a free table, and watch tonight's sales update live. Includes: overview, kitchen board, floor plan, reservations, menu, stock, staff, reports, settings, light and dark mode and a phone layout. How to use: it comes with sample data and no backend, and the dish photos are placeholders, so add your own photos and connect your API in src/store.ts. Built with TypeScript, HTML and CSS plus GSAP.
