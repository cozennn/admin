Tender

A pay button that swipes a card through itself

A Pay button that works like a card reader. Good for: checkout pages and online shops. How it works: press Pay and the card icon swipes through a slot, its line fills as a progress bar, and the button turns green with a check. How to use: copy the files into your checkout and run your own payment code on press, since payments are not included. No libraries needed.
