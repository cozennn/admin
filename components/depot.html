Depot

An order button where a van picks up your parcel

A checkout button that shows a small delivery scene when clicked. Good for: shop checkouts, place order buttons and delivery services. How it works: press it and a van drives in inside the button, loads a parcel and drives away. Then the button turns into a check mark. How to use: copy the files into your shop and connect the button to your own order step. No libraries needed.
