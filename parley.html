Parley

A customer messaging dashboard for SMS, WhatsApp and email

An eight-page dashboard for a business that talks to its customers by SMS, WhatsApp and email. Good for: a shop, a service business or a marketing tool. How it works: reply to customers in a shared inbox, send campaigns to groups of contacts, and set up automatic messages step by step. Includes: overview, inbox, campaigns, scheduled messages, audience, automations, reports, settings, search and a phone layout. How to use: it comes with sample data and no backend, so connect your own API in src/store.ts. Built with TypeScript, HTML and CSS plus GSAP.
