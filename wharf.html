Wharf

A stock and orders dashboard for small shops

A ten-page dashboard for running a small product business. Good for: online shops, small brands and wholesale businesses. How it works: receive a delivery and the stock level, status and overview all update, and move each order to its next step with one button. Includes: Overview, Orders, Stock, Reports, Products, Suppliers, Customers, Team, Settings and Help, light and dark mode and search, on desktop, tablet and phone. How to use: comes with sample data, so replace js/data.js with your own data or backend. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and uses GSAP for animation.
