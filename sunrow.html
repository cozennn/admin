Sunrow

A project app for solar and battery building companies

A business app for companies that build solar farms, battery storage and small power grids. Good for: construction and energy firms that run many building sites at once. How it works: you plan tasks on a timeline chart and drag a task to a new day, and the tasks that wait on it move too. Includes: a dashboard, projects, the schedule, contractors, materials, purchase orders, budgets, approvals, timesheets, safety reports, equipment, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data with no backend, so add your own data or server. Built with TypeScript and GSAP, no framework.
