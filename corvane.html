Corvane

A payments operations dashboard for card processors and fintech teams

A back-office dashboard for a company that takes card and bank payments for other businesses, shown with sample data from a small card processor. Good for: payments, risk and support teams, fintech founders and agencies building a processor's or marketplace's admin panel. How it works: the home screen shows money taken today against yesterday, a 30-day chart, what needs a person (held payments, disputes, new merchants, returned payouts) and a live payments feed, and the risk page lets you drag two thresholds across last month's risk scores to see how much fraud you would stop and how many good customers you would block before you apply them. Includes: a payments table with filters, CSV export, refunds and a timeline for each payment, a review queue to approve or block held payments with undo, a disputes board with evidence checklists and deadlines, payouts with instant payout and retry, merchant onboarding with identity and sanctions checks, reports, API keys that only show after you re-enter your password, webhooks with test sends, a request log, an audit log of every action, team roles, two-step sign-in, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data saved in the browser with no backend, so connect it to your own payments API and data. Built with React, TypeScript and GSAP.
